| Q23 | Which labels does a review record, and what does the reviewer see? | **Five labels** (2026-10-05), most serious first: *Security relevant* (needs a note: what was found), *Suspicious* (note: what to check next), *Operationally interesting*, *Expected behaviour*, *Insufficient evidence* (note: what is missing). No reviews had been recorded, so nothing was migrated. Q21's proposal updated. |

| Q39 | When is an amount of activity flagged (Phase 4g)? | **When it is unusual for that series at that time, not to fill a quota** (2026-10-28): robust deviation of log(1 + lines) in 5, 30, 120 and 720-minute windows, against the same weekday's training days at that clock time and against the series' level when active, the smaller counting; a fixed bar of z 3 with the validation budget only as a ceiling; the Isolation Forest a second opinion, alerting alone only above every training step's score. | Q40 | Should names be model features? | **No** (2026-10-28): server and agent names, usernames and source IPs are not features in Phase 3 or Phase 4g, nor is the source port a number. A model that learns names finds a new entity unusual for its name alone and overfits to the known ones. Names group the lines, give each entity its own baseline and trace a flag back; whether a name is new is the novelty and first-seen detectors' job. | Phase 3 re-run: OSSEC 2026-10-30; AUTH pending (README known gap 10). Analyze follows once retrained. |

AUTH 218 alerts → 42 items, OSSEC 14 → 10. |
